# The Tide Mother — Video Prompts for Grok Imagine (thriller-formula beats)

*170 image-to-video prompts, 27:59 total. Each clip is 6, 10 or 15 seconds, the lengths Grok Imagine handles best, chosen by scene type, and time-coded in three parts. Every speaking character has a unique voice, and every character on screen gets their own signature mannerism, so nobody moves the same way.*

**How to use in Grok Imagine**
- Upload the matching image beat, choose **Make video**, set **16:9**, set the clip length shown on the beat (6 s, 10 s or 15 s), and paste the prompt.
- Use **Normal** mode. Regenerate from the same image if a face or the dragon drifts.
- Keep each character's voice line identical across clips so Grok keeps the voice consistent; see `06-characters-voices-behaviors.md`.
- 15-second clips are only used for big climaxes and long dialogue. If your plan caps clips at 10 s, generate the first two parts, then **Extend** with the last part.
