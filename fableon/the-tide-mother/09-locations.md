# Location Bible — Kelvhaven (*The Tide Mother*)

Use this the same way as your character sheets. First generate the **master aerial image (L0)**, which is built from Beats 002–003. Then generate a **3-panel reference sheet** for each place, uploading L0 as the reference so every place belongs to the same village. When you make a beat image, attach the location sheet named in the beat's **Location** line together with the character sheets, so streets, houses and the harbor look the same in every shot.

**Rules for every location prompt:**
- The look comes from the Visual Style Profile, unchanged: photorealistic 35 mm film still, desaturated high-contrast grade, palette of storm slate grey, fog grey, driftwood brown and wet-wool grey.
- The period is a medieval northern fishing village, around 1100 AD: grey fieldstone, turf roofs, driftwood timber, rope, nets and tallow light. There is no glass in the windows (only wooden shutters), and no modern objects.
- The base sheets have **no people** and show an **overcast, wet early-winter day with no snow and no ice**. Night, storm and spring versions are made with the edit prompts at the end.

## The village map (keep it fixed)

Kelvhaven sits on a curved bay facing east, with black basalt cliffs on its **north** side.

1. **The harbor (L1)** is at the center of the bay: a long timber jetty, clinker-built rowboats, fish-drying racks and a squat stone **bell tower** at the root of the jetty.
2. **The harbor market (L2)** runs along the waterfront, just south of the jetty.
3. **The main lane (L3)** is a muddy cobbled lane climbing from the market, up between the cottages, to the square.
4. **The village square (L4)** is uphill, with the weathered **standing stone** at its center.
5. **Hesk's longhouse (L5)** stands at the top of the square: the oldest and longest house, with the moss-covered **sea-dragon carving** over its oak door.
6. **Bram's fishing shed (L6)** is a black-tarred timber shed at the end of the jetty, by the water.
7. **Maren's cottage (L7)** is the **last cottage at the north end** of the lane, the closest house to the cliffs, small and lonely.
8. **Odile's cottage (L8)** is the next one down the lane, about twenty steps away. Its front door looks straight at Maren's door.
9. **The cliff path (L9)** starts behind Maren's cottage and climbs the black cliffs to the north.
10. **The cliff edge (L10)** is at the top of the path, where the old **drowning stone** stands, with rope grooves worn into it. It is the same cliff where Maren gave birth.
11. **The hidden cove (L11)** is reached by a side path that drops down from the cliff path: a black-sand beach, tidepools and the mouth of the sea cave. You can't see it from the village, but from the beach you can see the distant village lights across the water.
12. **The sea cave (L12)** is inside the cove, a tidal cave of black basalt.
