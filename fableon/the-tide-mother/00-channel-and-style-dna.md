# FABLEON — Channel Brief, Research and Style DNA

## STATE 1–2 — Channel to clone and branding brief

**Source channel:** MYTHRA (@mythrafilms), an AI-generated dark-fantasy film channel with about 200K subscribers. Its "She Gave Birth to a Dragon | The Mothers Monster" reached 62M views in 2 weeks.

**Chosen clone channel name:** **FABLEON** (fable + eon)

**Other name variants:** SAGEON (saga + eon) · LUMERA (lumen + era) · NOCTERA (nocturne + era) · ORIGENA (origin + genesis + era)

**Channel description, variant 1:** Each film here is a story no one has told before. It starts as a single image in my mind and grows, scene by scene, into a world you can step inside, made by one person from the first idea to the last frame. FABLEON joins two words, fable and eon. Fables were passed down for ages. This is the age where I build my own.

**Channel description, variant 2:** Nothing here is borrowed. Every film starts as a quiet idea and becomes a full world, imagined, shaped and finished by one creator. LUMERA comes from lumen and era: the light that makes images, and the time we live in now. This is where I put that light into stories.

**Logo prompt:** Minimalist monogram logo, a single bold uppercase letter "F" in a refined geometric sans-serif with sharp, even strokes, warm ivory-cream color on a deep matte black circular background, soft dark vignette toward the edges, faint film-grain texture, no gradients on the letter, no extra elements, premium cinematic film-studio identity, centered composition, high contrast, elegant and timeless, 1:1 aspect ratio.

**Banner prompt:** Cinematic YouTube channel banner, 2560x1440, wide panoramic layout. The channel name "FABLEON" is centered in very large, widely letter-spaced uppercase geometric sans-serif, warm ivory-cream. Thin horizontal hairlines run out from both sides of the name to the edges of the frame. Below the name, a small tagline in widely spaced uppercase reads "WHERE LEGENDS TAKE SHAPE". The background is a smooth sepia-to-umber gradient with soft golden light glowing behind the text and fading into deep black at the corners, with a subtle film-grain and dust texture. The mood is moody, luxurious and mythic, like the opening title card of a prestige film studio. Keep all text within the central safe area, with no other imagery.

## STATE 5 — Style DNA

- **Niche:** AI-generated dark-fantasy short films (23–30 min) about a forbidden bond between an outcast human and a feared creature, set in a pre-modern village where superstition is law.
- **Target audience:** broad and global, many non-native English speakers. The story has to work with the sound off. Core viewers are women 25–55 (maternal sacrifice) and fantasy/creature fans 18–35. Lean-back TV and bedtime viewing.
- **Hook style:** a cold open in physical crisis with no context, then an impossible-reveal line at about 80 seconds. Formula: sensory chaos, then one shocking image, then a 4–6 word line that confirms it.
- **Script flow:** crisis birth (0–5%), rejection to bonding (5–12%), the lie (12–18%), feeding mystery (18–25%), suspicion and first sacrifice (25–33%), growth montage (33–40%), accuser goes to authority (40–45%), new power (45–50%), near-discovery (50–55%), blame event (55–60%), mob climax and the mother's loss (60–70%), creature returns (64–80%), echo montage (80–88%), quiet ending with a sequel hook (88–100%).
- **Sentence rhythm:** 3–6 words per line, many one-word lines, no line over about 12 words. Mostly imperatives and questions. A word repeated 3–6 times means panic. Silence is the main rhythm.
- **Tone:** somber, tender and tragic, a fairy tale played at grief level. Humor only in the bonding montages.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on sound, time skips shown through the creature's growth, a three-location loop, and optional narrator bridges only between acts.
- **Curiosity gaps:** What is it? What does it eat? Will the lie be exposed? How big will it get? Will it hurt them? Will it come back for her? The ending leaves an open question.
- **Emotional triggers:** maternal sacrifice, an innocent misjudged as a monster, mob injustice, loss of the protector, the creature's payoff. Injustice is the strongest one.
- **Retention techniques:** a new visual escalation every 2–3 minutes, callback lines, an antagonist who always has a concrete next step, an echo montage before the ending, a small cast with names repeated.
- **Direct address:** none. No fourth wall, no CTA inside the film.
- **Words per second:** about 0.4 for MYTHRA-style films.
- **Word count target:** 700 spoken words of dialogue ±5% (665–735). The final script has 692 dialogue words, plus 273 words of narration (prologue, act bridges, lesson epilogue) added in v2, for a 20:37 film timed with the thriller formula (hook 6 s, suspense 10 s, dialogue 10–15 s, reveal 6 s, climax 15 s, cliffhanger 6–10 s).
