## 4. The new script

## PART A — THE HUNT AT THE HARBOR
*Place: after Beat 160 · 26:22–33:22 · 7:00*


**26:22 — Harbor, night — one night's rest**

> **HESK:** Then let him rest here tonight. Let Kelvhaven see him in daylight.

> **MAREN:** One night. Then we go.

Tal curls up on the black shingle below the jetty. Maren falls asleep against his warm side.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** For one night, Kelvhaven slept beside a dragon. But not everyone slept.


**26:42 — Bram's shed, night — the poison**

In Bram's shed, the lamp is burning. Men crowd in.

> **BRAM:** You think it's tame? It's waiting. Tonight it smiles. Tomorrow night, it burns.

> **BRAM:** It'll come in the dark. Burn the boats. Burn the roofs. Turn Kelvhaven to dust while your children sleep.

> **JORY:** It saved her, Bram.

> **BRAM:** It saved its mother. Not you.

> **BRAM:** So we take it now. At first light. While it sleeps.

Bram pushes a longbow into Jory's hands.

> **BRAM:** Your father would have taken it. Be a man of Kelvhaven.

Bowstrings. Weighted nets. Arrows.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** Fear needs only one voice. Bram had a loud one.


**27:37 — Harbor, first light — the hunters**

First light. Maren wakes to the creak of bows.

Along the jetty and the beach, a line of villagers with nets, bows and harpoons.

Maren stands and spreads her arms in front of Tal.

> **MAREN:** What is this? He's sleeping. He's happy here.

> **MAREN:** He is not angry. He doesn't want to hurt you. He is one of us. Look at him!

Tal tilts his head, curious.

> **BRAM:** One of us? It's pretending, widow. Pretending until the dark comes.

> **JORY:** Bram... what if she's right?

> **BRAM:** Then we lose a net. If I'm right, we lose everything.

> **MAREN:** Listen to me. Stay clear of him. If he feels he isn't safe, he will fight back. And when he fights, I won't be able to stop him.

> **MAREN:** He's grown. I can't hold him anymore. Not if you hurt him.

> **BRAM:** Then step aside.

> **ODILE:** Maren, come away from it!

> **MAREN:** Him, Odile. Not it. Him.

> **BRAM:** Now!


**29:15 — Harbor — the hunt**

Weighted nets crash over Tal. Ropes pull tight. He looks at Maren, confused.

A volley of arrows arcs into the morning sky.

> **MAREN:** No! Stop!

An arrow pierces Tal's wing. His eyes change.

Tal rears up and tears the nets apart. Men are thrown into the water.

A harpoon flies at his head. Tal catches it in his jaws and snaps it like a twig.

His tail sweeps the jetty. Blue fire hits the boats, just as Bram said it would.

Blue fire catches Bram's sleeve. He plunges his burning arm into the sea, screaming.

The market stalls burst apart. Blue fire strikes the bell tower and the bell crashes down. Villagers run.

Maren runs through the chaos.

> **MAREN:** Tal! Tal, look at me!

He doesn't.

Maren gets too close. Tal's wing sweeps round without seeing her and throws her into the nets.

Jory is pinned under a fallen mast. Blue fire gathers in Tal's throat.

> **MAREN:** Not him! Tal, not him!

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** She had warned them. Now everything she warned them of was happening. And there was nothing she could do.


**31:13 — Harbor — the Tide Mother's cry**

Maren falls to her knees at the broken end of the jetty, at the edge of the sea.

She cries out. A loud, raw cry, the cry of a mother. It rolls across the whole bay.

A single tear falls from her cheek into the sea.

The tear touches the water. A ring of silver light spreads across the harbor.

Tal freezes mid-strike. The fire dies in his throat. He shudders, as if something has been torn out of him.

Thunder. The sky splits open. Heavy rain falls in sheets and puts out every fire.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** They say the sea keeps what it loves. That morning, Kelvhaven learned the sea also weeps for it.

Maren lifts her face to the rain. Thunder answers her. She understands: the sky is crying with her.

Tal lowers his head in the rain. His glow softens. He turns toward her voice.

Maren walks to him through the rain and lays her hand on his face.

> **MAREN:** Calm down, Tal. Calm down. Look at me. We are not like them. We are not like them.

Tal hums back. Three soft notes. He lays his head at her feet.

The rain eases. Villagers come out, soaked, and stare at what is left.

> **JORY:** She told us. She told us to stay clear.

Hesk arrives at the top of the lane, leaning on his staff. Too late.

Bram kneels in the shallows, holding his broken harpoon. He looks up at Maren.

> **BRAM:** This isn't over.

## PART B1 — PEACE, AND THE PLOT AGAINST IT
*Place: after Beat A42, before Beat 161 · 33:22–35:30 · 2:08*


**33:22 — Harbor, morning after the storm — Hesk's peace**

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** By morning, the rain had washed the smoke away. What it could not wash away was what everyone had seen.

Hesk stands among the burned boats.

> **HESK:** She warned us. We did not listen. That is twice now, Kelvhaven.

> **HESK:** He had every reason to burn us. At one cry from his mother, he stopped. I give you my word, as your elder. He will not harm you, if we do not harm him.

> **MAREN:** You would let him stay?

> **HESK:** I would let him belong.

A little girl holds out a fish. Tal takes it, gently. For the first time in days, Maren smiles.

At the edge of the crowd, Bram and four men do not smile.


**34:23 — Bram's shed, dusk — the conspiracy**

That evening, Maren walks past Bram's shed with a basket of fish. She hears voices. She stops.

> **BRAM:** Hesk has gone soft. The old fool wants us to feed it.

> **MAN:** Then what do we do?

> **BRAM:** Tonight, when it sleeps. Hemlock in the herring. Harpoons in the eyes. And the widow goes back to the sea, where we put her.

Through a gap in the planks, Maren's eye. She covers her mouth.

A board creaks under her foot.

> **BRAM:** Who's there?

Bram throws open the door. Nobody.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** Peace is a promise. And Maren had just heard how cheaply some men keep theirs.

Maren kneels beside Tal.

> **MAREN:** We can't stay, Tal. Not here. Not with them.

## PART B2 — SKARHOLM, THE ISLAND OF DRAGONS
*Place: after Beat 167, before Beat 168 · 36:32–39:46 · 3:14*


**36:32 — The open sea — following the roars**

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** They followed the roars for three days and three nights. Until the sea turned warm, and the sky turned to smoke.

An island of black rock rises out of the sea. Dragons circle its burning peak.

The largest of them comes down from the peak. The Ash Mother.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** Tal had found his own kind. That is what he believed. But creatures of one blood can have very different hearts. Different mothers. Different laws. Different ideas of what keeps them alive.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** These were dragons of the land and of the fire. And their memory of men was spears.

The Ash Mother sniffs the arrow wound in Tal's wing. Then she turns her gold eyes on Maren. And snarls.

> **MAREN:** He is my son. Wherever he goes, I go.

Tal curls his tail around her. The Ash Mother lets it be.


**37:39 — Skarholm — the lessons**

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** Tal wanted to learn how to live on the land. How to hunt without the sea. How to walk on stone.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** But every lesson had another lesson underneath it. Never forgive. Never forget. Whoever hurts you, you burn. Without mercy.

She shows him the graveyard. Dragon bones. And rusted harpoons still stuck in them.

Then the Ash Mother breathes. Not blue. Yellow. The rock melts like wax. Tal tries. Blue.

He tries again. Angrier. The blue flickers... and turns gold.

Close on Tal's eye. Cyan... turning gold.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** Blue fire warms. Yellow fire destroys. And Tal had just learned the difference.


**38:50 — Skarholm, five winters — Maren suspects**

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** One winter became two. Two became five. Maren mended nets alone, by a fire at the edge of the island.

She hums the three notes. Up on the ridge, Tal hears. For the first time, he doesn't answer.

At dawn, a fishing boat washes up. Burned. Yellow scorch marks.

> **MAREN:** Tal... what did you do?

> **MAREN:** Those were fishermen, Tal. Like your father.

Tal growls at her. For the first time in his life.

That night, while the dragons sleep, she climbs onto his back.

> **MAREN:** We're going home. Before I lose you for good.

They fly. On the peak, the Ash Mother watches them go. She does not stop them.

## PART B3 — THE RETURN
*Place: after Beat 171, before Beat 172 · 40:41–42:19 · 1:38*


**40:41 — Kelvhaven, spring, five years later — the return**

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** Five years. Long enough to rebuild a harbor. Long enough to forget your fear. But not long enough.

Jory spots a shape on the horizon. A girl shouts.

> **CHILD:** It's Tal! Tal is coming home!

> **HESK:** She kept her word. Five winters, and not one boat lost.

Tal sweeps in low over the harbor. Bigger. Darker. Glowing gold. The smiles fade.

He lands on the new jetty. It cracks under him. His gold eyes sweep the crowd... and stop on Bram.

Bram backs away.

> **BRAM:** I told you. I told all of you.

Tal opens his jaws. Yellow fire builds in his throat.

> **MAREN:** Tal, no! These are our people!

Yellow fire. The rebuilt bell tower explodes. Stone melts. Kelvhaven screams.

Maren falls to her knees and cries out. A tear falls into the sea. And nothing happens.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.):** This time, the sea did not answer.

> **HESK:** Mother of the tide... what have you brought back to us?

Tal turns his gold eyes on the village. And roars.
