# Characters, Voices and Behaviors — *The Tide Mother*

Each character has a **unique voice** (paste the voice line unchanged into Grok every time they speak, so the voice stays consistent) and **their own physical habits**. The video prompts give each on-screen character one habit per clip, rotating through the list, so no two characters move the same way and nobody repeats the same gesture in every shot.

---

## NARRATOR (voice-over only)
- **Voice prompt:** a deep, resonant, unhurried older male storyteller with warm gravel and a faint Nordic lilt, like telling a legend by a fire.
- **Delivery:** long pauses before reveals, and he drops to near-whisper on the last words of each act bridge.

## MAREN — the Tide Mother
- **Look:** about 30, long wet tangled dark-brown hair with a thin braid on the left, freckles, grey-green eyes, faded indigo wool dress, moss-green shawl, rope belt with a bone net-mending needle.
- **Voice prompt:** soft husky alto with a gentle northern-coastal lilt, breathy when afraid, sentences trailing off quietly.
- **Signature behaviors:**
  - twists the thin braid on the left when anxious
  - touches the bone net-needle at her belt for comfort
  - lifts her chin when defiant
  - hums three soft notes of an old sea lullaby (this hum returns at the end)
- **Never:** shouts at Tal. She commands him quietly.

## TAL — the dragon son
- **Look:** baby (cat-sized, translucent pearl skin, huge dark-blue eyes) → juvenile (pony-sized, teal shimmer, glowing throat) → adult (fishing-boat-sized, manta-fin wings, cyan eyes, glowing veins).
- **Vocal signature:**
  - baby: crystalline dolphin-like chirps and clicks
  - juvenile: a warbling whale-calf hum
  - adult: a deep whale-song roar with a bell-like ring
- **Signature behaviors:**
  - **tilts his head to the left** when curious, at every age (viewers will recognize him by it)
  - puffs out his cheeks, a callback to "Hold your breath"
  - curls his tail around Maren
  - his glow pulses brighter with emotion

## ODILE — the neighbor
- **Look:** about 45, grey-streaked auburn hair under a linen headscarf, ruddy cheeks, oatmeal apron over a brown wool dress.
- **Voice prompt:** quick, chattering, high sing-song voice, with little gasps between phrases.
- **Signature behaviors:**
  - wrings her apron in both hands
  - touches two fingers to her lips and then toward the sea (Kelvhaven's superstitious sign)
  - fusses with the knot of her linen headscarf
  - quick, fluttering hand gestures
- **Arc:** her gossip starts the danger, and she's the first to say "We were wrong."

## BRAM — the fish-trader
- **Look:** burly, about 40, scarred jaw, matted red-brown beard, oiled leather fisherman's smock, sealskin vest.
- **Voice prompt:** low gravelly bass, clipped and sneering, rolled R's, words spat out.
- **Signature behaviors:**
  - drums his thick fingers on whatever is near
  - cracks his knuckles
  - leans in uncomfortably close
  - spits on the ground

## HESK — the elder
- **Look:** about 70, long white beard, wooden staff carved with wave patterns, heavy grey hooded cloak.
- **Voice prompt:** very slow, deep, wheezing old voice with long pauses between words, formal and cold.
- **Signature behaviors:**
  - **taps his staff twice before he speaks**
  - strokes his long white beard
  - closes his eyes instead of looking at anyone when he judges

## JORY — the young fisherman (speaks the VILLAGER lines)
- **Look:** lanky, about 18, messy blond hair, grey wool cap.
- **Voice prompt:** a nasal, cracking teenage voice that stammers when scared.
- **Signature behaviors:** fidgets and tugs at his grey cap.
- **Arc:** first to blame the storm, first to see that Tal saved her.

## Season map and wardrobe (whole film)
The story moves through three seasons. Every image and video prompt carries a **Season** line and dresses the characters for it. The two season changes are announced by the narrator, so viewers always know where they are in time. Voices and behaviors never change.

| Part | Beats | Time | Weather on screen | Narrated transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Start of winter: the great storm** | 1–62 | 0:00–10:17 | Cold rain, sleet, sea spray, grey skies; no snow yet | The prologue: *"Then came the winter of the great storm."* |
| **B. Deep winter** | 63–168 | 10:17–27:34 | Snow falling, snow on roofs, rocks and boats, a harbor frozen at the edges, icicles; visible breath vapor whenever anyone speaks or breathes (except underwater) | **Beat 63 (15 s, new):** *"The great storm was only winter knocking. Weeks later, it walked in. Overnight, snow buried the boats, the roofs, the paths. The harbor froze at its edges. And the fish stopped coming."* |
| **C. Spring, years later** | 169–171 | 27:34–28:19 | Snow melting off the roofs, dripping icicles, fresh green grass, the first sea-thrift buds | **Beat 169 (15 s, new):** *"Winters came and went. The ice broke. And when spring finally returned to Kelvhaven, it found a village that was learning to see the sea differently."* |

Beat 168 (someone leaves a fish at the cave mouth "every winter since") stays in winter on purpose. It shows a later winter, just before the spring transition.

### A. Start of winter: original costumes (from the Visual Style Profile character bible, unchanged)
- **MAREN:** faded indigo wool dress, moss-green knitted shawl, rope belt with net-mending needle.
- **ODILE:** linen headscarf, oatmeal apron over a brown wool dress.
- **BRAM:** oiled leather fisherman's smock, sealskin vest.
- **HESK:** heavy grey hooded cloak, staff carved with wave patterns.
- **VILLAGERS:** layered undyed wool and oilskins.

### B. Deep winter costumes
- **MAREN:** long **black** wool winter dress, heavy black hooded wool cloak lined with grey sheepskin, black wool leg-wraps, fur-lined leather boots, rope belt with the net-mending needle. She keeps the moss-green shawl only until she wraps it around Tal at the start of winter; after that she goes without it, which matches Odile's line "Your shawl is gone."
- **TAL:** unchanged design, with frost on his scales above water. As a juvenile he wears Maren's moss-green shawl around his neck after she gives it to him.
- **ODILE:** **faded red** wool winter dress, thick faded red hooded wool shawl over her linen headscarf, oatmeal apron, sheepskin-lined boots.
- **BRAM:** all **black**: black knitted wool cap, heavy black oiled sealskin coat with a black fur collar, black wool trousers, black leather boots, frost in his beard.
- **HESK:** **animal colors**: a heavy cloak of brown and grey wolf fur over a tan deer-hide tunic, sheepskin hood, deer-hide boots, his carved staff.
- **JORY and the VILLAGERS:** layered undyed wool, sheepskin vests, fur-trimmed hoods, knitted wool caps, mittens, wool leg-wraps and fur boots.

### C. Spring costumes (epilogue)
- **ODILE:** a **faded red** linen spring dress with rolled sleeves, a light faded red wool shawl, linen headscarf and oatmeal apron. Same color as her winter look, so viewers still recognize her.
- **VILLAGERS and CHILDREN:** undyed linen shirts and dresses, light wool vests, bare heads; the children barefoot.
