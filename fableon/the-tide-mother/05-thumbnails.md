**Thumbnail system (from the STATE 10 analysis, recolored for FABLEON):**
- Movie-poster title lockup instead of a hook: a classical Roman serif in all caps (Trajan-like), with a small spaced "THE" above, a stacked title, a chiselled metallic fill and a dark drop shadow.
- A muted, cold world with one saturated creature color (Tal's pearl-teal and electric cyan-blue) and **one warm emotional accent** (blood red, or mob-torch orange).
- Two subjects in frame: Maren close-up on the left, the much larger Tal on the right, with eye contact.
- A gold "4K UHD" badge in the top-right corner on full-movie thumbnails.

**Common style suffix for every prompt:**
`photorealistic cinematic movie-poster key art, dark fantasy, 16:9 YouTube thumbnail 1280x720, ultra-sharp focus on faces and eyes, shallow depth of field, desaturated cold background, high contrast, film grain, dramatic rim light`

**Character lock (paste unchanged):**
- **Maren:** a woman about 30, long wet tangled dark-brown hair with a thin braid on the left, pale freckled skin, grey-green eyes, faded indigo wool dress, moss-green knitted shawl.
- **Tal (adult):** a colossal sea dragon with iridescent pearl-grey and abalone-teal scales, deep sea-blue manta-fin wings, a crown of frilled spines, luminous cyan slit-pupil eyes, bioluminescent blue veins.
- **Tal (baby):** a cat-sized newborn sea dragon, translucent pearl-grey wet skin, oversized glassy dark-blue eyes, tiny fin wings, a soft frilled crest.

**Text tip:** most image models misspell text. Generate each image with the text area left clean, then add the title in post with Cinzel or Trajan Pro, using a chiselled metallic gradient (ivory #F2EBDD to steel-teal #6FB7B1, or ivory to crimson #9E1B1B) and a 70% black drop shadow.
