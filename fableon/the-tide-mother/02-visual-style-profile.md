# FABLEON Visual Style Profile v2 — *The Tide Mother* (colorful upgrade)

v1 copied MYTHRA's desaturated look too closely, so the frames felt dull. v2 keeps the cinematic mood and shadows but adds **vivid, eye-catching color accents** in every frame and builds Kelvhaven into a memorable village, so every frame has something to look at.

## 1. Art style (in every prompt)
`cinematic photorealistic dark-fantasy film still, shot on 35mm film, fine grain, shallow depth of field, rich cinematic color grade with vivid jewel-tone accents glowing against moody shadows, high dynamic range, 16:9`

**Rule of three colors:** every frame has (1) a moody base (slate blue, black basalt, driftwood), (2) one **warm** accent (amber lantern, saffron paint, torch orange, sunrise gold) and (3) one **cool, magical** accent (Tal's cyan, aurora green and violet, sea-glass teal, glowing plankton).

## 3. The Kelvhaven build (the community)
- **Founding story shown on screen:** shipwreck survivors who "painted their boats the colors of the dawn that saved them". Every boat is painted faded red, ochre/saffron or sea-glass teal, which gives the harbor instant color.
- **Homes:** grey fieldstone cottages with **turf roofs blooming with pink sea-thrift and yellow gorse**, doors and shutters painted red, saffron and teal, and **sea-glass mobiles** in windows throwing colored light.
- **Landmarks** (repeat them so viewers learn the map):
  1. The **whalebone arch** over the harbor entrance.
  2. **Elder Hesk's longhouse**, with a huge **weathered sea-dragon carving** over the door and on the central beam. This foreshadows that the village once honored dragons.
  3. The **standing stone** in the square, carved with dragon spirals.
  4. The **drowning stone** at the cliff edge, with deep rope grooves.
  5. The **bell tower** on the harbor.
- **Life and movement in every wide shot:** dyed wool ribbons snapping on poles, silver fish on drying racks, amber horn lanterns, gulls, chimney smoke, children running.
- **Market:** striped canvas awnings in red and saffron, baskets of silver herring, orange crabs and green kelp, painted signs.

## 4. Lighting v2
- Day: a pearl-grey sky **broken by shafts of golden sun**, never flat grey.
- Dawn: a pink-and-gold sunrise through mist.
- Night: **the northern lights rippling green and violet**, plus Tal's cyan glow and **bioluminescent plankton** in the surf. Orange torches mean danger.
- Interiors: one warm source (candle, peat fire), with colored light from sea-glass windows.

## 5. Eye-candy moments ("glue to screen" shots)
- An aerial prologue over the golden sea into the painted harbor.
- Aurora over the sleeping village, swallowed by storm clouds.
- Cyan plankton in the rock pools during the birth.
- Jewel-toned tide pools (orange starfish, purple urchins) in the growth montage.
- Blue fire rolling over a plankton-lit cove under the aurora.
- Torches against the aurora at the cave mouth.
- A turquoise underwater rescue with glowing plankton.
- Blue fire lighting the whole harbor.
- A pink-gold dawn flight over the ocean.

## 6. Camera
- Shot mix: about 40% close/extreme close, 35% medium, 25% wide or aerial.
- Low angles on Tal; slow push-ins on emotion; handheld for chaos; slow motion for the fall and the fire.
- Each 15-second Grok clip has one continuous move: a glide, push, arc, crane or tilt.
