Kirkolt — Production Bible

Runtime: ~82s | Clips: 14 | Locations: 2

1. Characters

* Investigator Smith — lead investigator, 44. Tall (6'1"), lean-muscular build, close-cropped dark hair greying at the temples, tired hazel eyes with faint under-eye shadows, light stubble, fair-olive skin, a thin old scar through the left eyebrow. Office: rumpled white dress shirt with sleeves rolled to the forearm, loosened navy tie pulled low, charcoal trousers, ID badge clipped to breast pocket, brown leather wristwatch. Park (night): same shirt and tie, plus a dark grey wool overcoat with the collar turned up. Obsessively pursuing the Kirkolt disappearances; skeptical, worn down by pressure, and desperate for a real lead.
* Hannah — former police superintendent, 58. Tall, upright posture, silver-grey hair in a neat low chignon, sharp pale blue-grey eyes, fine lines at the eyes and mouth, fair skin, a composed and guarded expression. Charcoal-grey knee-length wool coat over a cream silk blouse, dark tailored trousers, low block-heel boots, thin grey scarf, brown leather satchel bag (holds the envelope). Same outfit in both scenes — she arrives at the office already dressed for the cold and goes straight to the park after. A whistleblower who risks her position to hand Smith 40 years of case files.

2. Locations

* Smith's office — int., evening into night, rain streaking the window, single desk lamp, blinds casting slatted shadows, a case board behind the desk covered in photos of missing men connected with red string and pins, cluttered files.
* Harold Park — ext., night (11:30 p.m.), foggy, one lone sodium-vapor lamp post lighting a bench and gravel path, distant blurred city lights beyond the tree line, otherwise empty and silent.

6. Aesthetic Direction

A slow-burn neo-noir crime-thriller mood, grounded and restrained rather than stylized. Low-key, motivated lighting throughout — a single desk lamp and cold rain-light in the office, one sodium-vapor lamp post cutting through fog at the park — with deep, detailed shadow rather than black crush. Camera is measured and mostly static or slow push-ins, never frantic. Wardrobe and sets stay lived-in and unglamorous: a working detective's cluttered office, an empty municipal park at night. Faces read as tired and guarded; tension comes from restraint and quiet, not spectacle. Must stay identical across characters, locations, stills, and video.

7. Color Grading

Yes — one grade applied to all 14 stills before video. Cool neo-noir teal-and-amber grade: teal-leaning shadows and midtones, warm amber highlights isolated to practical lamp sources, crushed-but-detailed blacks, moderate-to-high contrast, desaturated overall palette apart from protected warm skin tones, subtle vignette, visible fine film grain, faint blue-grey haze on the exterior park shots.
